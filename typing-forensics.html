    A demonstration of client-side automation detection. The hard tells (untrusted events, missing key code, text without a keystroke, Shift mismatch, navigator.webdriver) catch autotypers and naive scripted input with near certainty — including the Windows SendInput/VK_PACKET approach used by the autotyper in this repo. The heuristics (rhythm, entropy, rollover, burst structure, environment fingerprint) catch a bot that forges clean events but can't fake how a human actually behaves. None is proof on its own: a developer with DevTools open, an on-screen keyboard, IME composition, or assistive tech can trip a check, and a well-built CDP framework can satisfy the event-level ones — which is why the risk score weights many independent signals rather than trusting any single one.
